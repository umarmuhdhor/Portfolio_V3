What the tool is

Ordinary Explained Publisher is a private tool that Ordinary Explained uses to schedule and publish its own short explainer videos to its own social media accounts, including TikTok. It is not a public service: no one else can sign up, and no other accounts are connected to it.

Use

The tool is used only by Ordinary Explained, only for content Ordinary Explained owns or has the right to publish, and in line with the terms and community guidelines of each platform it posts to, including TikTok's Terms of Service and Community Guidelines.

Platform connections

Connecting a TikTok account lets the tool read that account's basic profile, statistics and public video list and publish videos to it, as described in the Privacy Policy. Access can be removed at any time from TikTok's settings under Security and permissions, or by disconnecting the account in the tool.

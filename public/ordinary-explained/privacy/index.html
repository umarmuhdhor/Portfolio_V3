Privacy Policy

Ordinary Explained Publisher · Last updated 11 November 2026

Ordinary Explained Publisher is a private tool run by Ordinary Explained, a channel of short explainer videos. It is used only to schedule and publish Ordinary Explained's own videos to its own social media accounts, including TikTok. It is not offered to the public and no other people's accounts are connected to it.

What we access

When the Ordinary Explained TikTok account authorises the tool, TikTok shares the following with it:

  • Basic profile information: open ID, display name, username and avatar.
  • Profile and statistics information: profile link, bio, verification status, and follower, following, like and video counts.
  • The list of the account's public videos and their view, like, comment and share counts.

The tool also uploads the videos and captions that Ordinary Explained chooses to publish.

How we use it

This information is used only to publish Ordinary Explained's videos at the scheduled time, to confirm that a post went through, and to show Ordinary Explained its own performance figures. It is not used for advertising, profiling or any other purpose.

Storage and sharing

Access tokens and the information above are stored on a self-hosted server controlled by Ordinary Explained. They are not sold, rented or shared with anyone. They are sent only to TikTok, as needed to make the requests described above.

Retention and removal

Information is kept only while the TikTok account stays connected. Disconnecting the account in the tool, or removing the app's access in TikTok's settings under Security and permissions, stops all access, and the stored tokens are then deleted.
